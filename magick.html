Introduction to Magick

What is Magick?

I will give it to you straight:

Magick is the mysticism as practiced in the so called "west". It is a way to interface with anything beyond the material world and ultimately, reach enlighenment.

Why Magick matters

People are already very much accustomed to Buddhism, Hinduism, Nirvana, Tantra, Gurus, etc. Well, it turns out we have our own thing. Our own path. The problem is that this path has been hidden, shunned and misrepresented for centuries. So much so, that a spiritually inclined person in the west today will naturally gravitate towards other practices - chief of which Buddhism - practices in which a westerner has no grounding, no cultural context. And so, an already difficult envdevour such as enlightenment, is made magnitudes more difficult.
